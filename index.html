About Us

Built to Transform Africa's Mobility

Our Story Vision & Mission Why We Exist Our Values

A Company Born from Necessity, Built with Ambition

KineticGrid Africa began with a simple, urgent observation: Africa is urbanizing faster than any continent in history, yet the infrastructure being built to support this growth is still largely dependent on fossil fuels and imported technology.

Our founders recognized that the transition to clean transportation was not just an environmental responsibility — it was an economic imperative and a generational opportunity.

KineticGrid Africa is headquartered in Nigeria, serving the entire African market with full-lifecycle, turnkey new-energy mobility solutions for governments, transit authorities, fleet operators and climate investment funds.

The Future We Are Committed to Building

Our Vision: A continent where every vehicle runs on clean energy, every city breathes clean air, and every African community benefits from the wealth created by sustainable infrastructure.

Our Mission: To accelerate Africa's transition to clean transportation by developing world-class EV infrastructure, renewable energy systems and smart mobility technology.

The Problem Only Infrastructure Can Solve

The challenge of EV adoption in Africa is not primarily about vehicle cost or consumer desire — it is about infrastructure confidence. Buyers will not purchase electric vehicles until they trust that charging infrastructure exists.

KineticGrid Africa's role is to break this cycle by deploying infrastructure ahead of demand, building the confidence that unlocks mass adoption.
